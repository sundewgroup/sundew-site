Good to know

FAQs

Where does my service take place?

All Sundew sessions are offered by private appointment and take place in your home, hotel, or bridal suite. We bring everything needed for a seamless, mess-free experience. Currently serving Highland Park, Turtle Creek, and select Dallas neighborhoods.

How should I prepare for my appointment?
  • Exfoliate 24–48 hours before.
  • Shave or wax at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Avoid lotion, deodorant, and perfume the day of.
  • Wear loose, dark clothing.

Pro tip: schedule nails, facials, or waxing before your tan—not after.

What should I wear during the service?

Whatever makes you feel most comfortable. Most clients go nude or wear a disposable thong. Male clients must wear undergarments.

When should I book before an event?

We recommend booking 1–2 days before your event.

Brides: schedule your trial tan at least 3 weeks before the big day.

What skincare should I use after my tan?
  • Moisturize daily with fragrance-free lotion.
  • Avoid oils, exfoliants, or long steamy showers.
  • Use a tan extender to help your glow last even longer.
Can you come to hotels or bridal suites?

Yes. Sundew serves private homes, hotels, and bridal suites across Highland Park, Turtle Creek, and select Dallas neighborhoods.
