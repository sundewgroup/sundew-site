01   The gap

She is in town for the most photographed weekend of her year. Your house can arrange her hair, her nails, her car and her table. It cannot arrange her tan.

Today she has three options.
None of them are yours.

  1. i.

    Leave the property

    An hour in a strip-mall salon, in the middle of a weekend you planned to the minute.

  2. ii.

    Let a stranger in

    An unvetted vendor from a search result, carrying equipment through your lobby and into your suite.

  3. iii.

    Go without

    And remember, in every photograph, the one thing nobody could arrange.

02   The concept

Sundew is the fourth option.

A named sunless service that lives inside your house: requested through your team, performed in the guest's own suite, and credited to you. The category did not exist in Dallas. We built it.

  • In suite

    A custom airbrush tan in the guest's room, inside an enclosed tent. No treatment room, no trip, no trace.

  • Under your name

    On your menu, booked through your concierge, spa desk or planner. The guest thanks you.

  • On call

    One message from your team. One dedicated artist who knows your property and your standards.
