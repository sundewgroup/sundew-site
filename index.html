The Sundew standard

How we work inside your house

  • One artist

    Every guest is cared for by the same hands. Sundew accepts a limited number of houses so that never changes.

  • Discretion

    Private appointments only. Your guests are never named, photographed, or discussed.

  • Nothing left behind

    An enclosed tent, protected surfaces, and everything carried in and out. The suite is left as it was found.

  • Custom to the guest

    Each tan is mixed for the guest's skin, the occasion, and the light they will be seen in.

  • Timed to the occasion

    One to two days before an event, with a trial for brides, so the color is at its best when it matters.

  • Your name first

    Guests book through you and thank you. Sundew works as part of your service, under your standards.

Beginning

How an appointment begins

  1. An introduction

    Tell us about your house and the guests you look after. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. A walk-through

    We visit, meet your team, and agree where and how Sundew will work on your property.

  3. A first season

    A trial period at no cost to the house, so your team and your guests can judge the service.

  4. In residence

    Sundew is offered as your own, with a single point of contact for your team.

For your team

Questions houses ask

What does Sundew need from the property?

A clean, well-lit space with room for a tanning tent, most often the guest's own suite. We bring everything else.

How do guests book?

Through your concierge, spa desk, or planner, who contacts Sundew directly. Guests never need to leave your care to arrange it.

What does it cost the house?

Nothing to begin. Guests may be charged a house rate, or the house may host the service as an amenity. We agree the arrangement with you during the walk-through.

How far ahead should a guest be booked?

We recommend one to two days before an event. Brides should schedule a trial tan at least three weeks before the wedding.

Where does Sundew work?

Sundew serves hotels, private residences, and bridal suites across Highland Park, Turtle Creek, and select Dallas neighborhoods.

Do you see private clients?

Yes, by private appointment. Private client services are here.
